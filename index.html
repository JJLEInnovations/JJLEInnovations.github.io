Mail Assistant

An assistant for Apple Mail, for people with a lot of it.

Apple Mail can tell you how much space an account or a mailbox is using. It cannot tell you which senders are behind it. Mail Assistant does: every sender ranked by how many messages they have sent and how much room they take, what is arriving week by week, who has gone quiet, and who you have never once opened.

Then it lets you act on the answer — search every account together, and file, trash, mark or unsubscribe hundreds of messages in a single pass. Nothing changes until you confirm it, Apple Mail carries out every change, and what you move can be put back.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Privacy · Support
