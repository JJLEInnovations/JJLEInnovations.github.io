Mail Assistant

Manage every Apple Mail account like never before.

See who is really filling your mailbox, ranked by how many messages they send and how much space they take. Search all your accounts at once — by sender, subject, or words inside a message. Then file, trash, or mark hundreds of messages in a single pass, across every account at the same time.

Apple Mail makes you do that one sender and one mailbox at a time. This does not. Nothing changes until you confirm it, and what you move can be put back.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Privacy · Support
