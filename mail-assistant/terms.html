2. How the App Works

The App operates on the mail that Apple Mail has already downloaded to your Mac. It reads that mail locally and instructs Apple Mail to carry out the actions you confirm. Because those actions are performed by Apple Mail and your mail provider, certain results are outside our control. In particular:

  • Actions are performed by Apple Mail. When you confirm an action, such as moving messages to Trash or marking them as read, the App sends the request to Apple Mail. Whether the action completes depends on Apple Mail, your mail server and your internet connection, none of which we control. On accounts that sync with a mail server, the changes also apply on your other devices and in webmail.
  • Messages in Trash may be permanently deleted. Depending on their settings, Apple Mail or your mail provider may permanently erase messages in Trash after a period of time. Once that happens, the App cannot recover them.
  • Restore is limited. Restore reverses only a Move to Trash, and only for messages that remain in Trash and that the App recorded as moved. Restore is offered only for your most recent actions. Any message that cannot be restored is listed as skipped, together with the reason. Moves to other folders and changes to a message's read or unread status are not covered by Restore, but you can reverse them yourself by moving the messages back or marking them again.
  • Accounts that use labels. Some providers, including Gmail, organize mail with labels rather than folders. On those accounts, moving a message to a folder may apply a label without removing the message from its original location, and moving a message to Trash removes it from every label it carries. The App displays a warning before performing either action.
  • Stopping an action in progress. If you press Stop while an action is running, messages that Apple Mail has already begun processing may still be moved or marked. You should review the affected mailbox afterwards.
  • Recent Actions is not a complete record. The App keeps a history of your confirmed actions in order to display Recent Actions and to support Restore. Older entries are removed automatically, and the history is erased if you clear it. It is not a complete or permanent record of changes to your mail and is not intended to serve as evidence.
  • Search results may be incomplete. Search results, counts and reports are drawn from Apple Mail's index and from the App's own search index. Either may be incomplete or out of date, for example while Apple Mail is still downloading messages.
