Mail Assistant

Manage every Apple Mail account like never before — without your mail ever leaving your Mac.

Everything stays on this Mac. Mail Assistant has no network permission, no account, and no analytics. It reads the mail Apple Mail already downloaded and never sends any of it anywhere.

What it does

See who is actually filling your inbox

The Senders Report ranks every sender by how many messages they have sent you and how much space those take. Sorting by size is usually the surprise: a handful of senders account for most of a mailbox.

Search across every account at once

By sender, subject, date, folder, attachment name, or words inside the message body. Apple Mail searches one mailbox at a time; this searches all of them together, from an index kept on your Mac.

Act on hundreds of messages in one pass

Move to a folder, move to Trash, or mark read or unread — for several senders at once, across different accounts. Every action shows a confirmation naming exactly how many messages it will affect, and can be stopped while it runs.

Put it back

Restore returns messages from Trash to the folders they came from. Mark as Read is undone by Mark as Unread. Recent actions keeps a record of everything you did.

Requirements

macOS 14 or later, and Apple Mail signed in to at least one account. Mail Assistant works alongside Apple Mail rather than replacing it — your mail stays exactly where it is.
